Mark alone: favicon, small screens

  • Strong and readable. The pin in the ring says "places" at a glance, which suits a blog organized by place.
  • The mark works on its own. The pin and ring can be the browser tab icon, the app icon and the social avatar, all from the same file.
  • One color adjustment: the bright blue is fine for the logo and icons, but it's too light for small text on white (3.4:1, where 4.5:1 is needed). Links use a deeper version of the same blue.
  • The dashed underline looks like a route on a map. It's reused below as the dividers between sections.

Works well

  • Very personal and charming.
  • It feels like your own notebook.
  • Memorable on social media.

Watch for

  • Three font families plus textures make pages slower.
  • Handwriting fonts are hard to read for some people, such as those with dyslexia or low vision. They can only be used for short notes.
  • An odd fit for advice posts like visas and taxes.
  • Decorative styles date quickly.

Recommendation: A
